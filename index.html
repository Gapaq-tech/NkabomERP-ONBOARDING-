FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to install anything?

No. NkabomERP runs in your web browser. Open your business address on any laptop, tablet or phone and log in.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live within a day. Larger setups with many branches or products can take a little longer, and we'll tell you upfront.

Can I accept Mobile Money?

Yes. MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash and AirtelTigo Money are built in, and payments are recorded against your invoices.

Is my business data safe?

Each business gets its own separate system and address. Connections are encrypted with HTTPS and your data is backed up regularly.

What if my internet is slow?

The system is light and works on ordinary mobile data. You only need a connection when you're using it.

Can I add more staff or branches later?

Yes. Message us whenever you grow and we'll add staff logins, branches or features to your system.
